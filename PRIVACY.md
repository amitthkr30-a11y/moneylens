# Privacy Policy — MoneyLens India (V1)

- **Local-only processing.** Statements are parsed in your browser; transactions are stored in your browser's IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to the app authors.
- **What is stored:** transactions (date, narration, amounts, balance), last 4 digits of account numbers, your budgets, loans, net-worth entries, settings, an audit log of actions (no financial values).
- **Never collected:** net-banking credentials, OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV. PDF passwords are used only in memory to open the file.
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages serves the static files and may log standard request metadata (IP, user agent) under GitHub's privacy statement. No cookies, analytics or trackers are used by the app.
- **Your controls:** export everything (Settings → Export JSON / Reports → CSV/Excel), delete a statement (Accounts), edit/ignore transactions, delete all data (Settings → Delete all data), or clear the site's browser data.
- **Account Aggregator (future):** consent screens will show purpose, data range, frequency and expiry; consent can be revoked anytime.
- **Children:** not intended for users under 18.
