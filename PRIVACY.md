# Privacy
- **Where data is processed.** Statements are processed in your browser.
- **Where data is stored.** Data stays on your device unless you turn on Cloud Sync. With Cloud Sync on, it's stored in your own Supabase project and only your account can read it.
- **What is never collected.** Bank logins, OTP, PINs and CVV.
- **What is masked.** Account and loan numbers are reduced to their last 4 digits.
- **What is not extracted.** Borrower name, address and CKYC number.
- **Export and delete.** Export everything from Settings. Delete statements from Accounts & Statements. Erase this device from Settings, and the cloud copy from Cloud Sync.
